FAQ

1. ITEM DETAILS

Q. What is a Japanese movie flyer (Chirashi)?
A. It is a B5-sized (approx. 7.1 x 10.1 inches) promotional poster distributed at movie theaters in Japan. They are unique to Japan and highly valued by collectors worldwide.
Q. Are the items authentic?
A. Yes. All items, including movie merchandise, art items, and apparel, are 100% authentic and shipped directly from Japan.
Q. How do I check the condition of an item?
A. We personally select each piece with care and take the photos ourselves. For pre-owned items, please review the listing photos before purchase. Please note that minor wear to an outer box may occur during international transit, which is not considered a defect.

2. PACKAGING & SHIPPING

Q. How do you pack fragile items like flyers and ticket stubs?
A. Paper items are shipped flat with a sturdy backing board and a protective clear sleeve to prevent bending. Other items are also carefully packed with suitable materials for safe delivery.
Q. Do you combine shipping for multiple items?
A. Yes. If you purchase multiple items in a single order, we will combine them into one package for safe and efficient delivery.

3. ORDERS & REQUESTS

Q. Can I request a specific movie title or item?
A. Yes. If you are looking for a specific title or brand not currently listed, please reach out to us through the Contact page. We will do our best to find it for you.
